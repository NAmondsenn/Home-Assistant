# 0010: A stronger wake word while something plays in the room

**Context.** Speech from a film or a video can sound enough like "Hey Nova" to wake it, and Nova then answers whatever the film says next. The user has no TV: they watch on their PC, which reports to Home Assistant through HASS.Agent, in the Bedroom, where the satellite is. They'll turn on HASS.Agent's media player. A read-only look on 30 Oct found HA's media players: Jellyfin's sessions (`media_player.firefox_20`, playing, no room and no device, a new one per session), and Spotify's (`media_player.spotify_nathan`, no room). Neither HA nor the satellite reads `wake_word_confidence` from the page today: the satellite uses its own config.yaml (0.5 on both machines).

**Decision.**

1. **What counts.** A media player Home Assistant puts in the satellite's room (its own area, else its device's - each satellite is a device of its own in HA, with its own room) whose state is `playing`. Paused, idle, off and unavailable don't count. Found at run time from HA, so a TV or speaker added later counts by itself. Nova's own music doesn't count: a player on any satellite's own HA device, or a Spotify player whose `source` is a connected satellite's Spotify speaker (e.g. "Voice-Assistant").
2. **How it's known.** The brain asks HA every 3 seconds, with one template, which media players are playing, with their rooms. A change is sent to every connected satellite straight away. If HA can't be read, nothing counts as playing (the normal wake word), and that's logged once, not every 3 seconds. Until a satellite's room is known, nothing counts for it. Each satellite goes by its own room.
3. **A stronger wake word.** The satellite's state message gains `wake_confidence`: the normal setting, or the stronger one while something plays. Two settings on the page under Listening, used straight away: "How sure Nova must be it heard its wake word" (`thresholds.wake_word_confidence`, 0.5) and "...while something's playing in the room" (`thresholds.media_wake_word_confidence`, 0.75). The higher of the two is used while something plays, so the second can never make Nova easier to wake. A satellite which isn't told keeps its own config.yaml's (an older brain, or the brain unreachable).
4. **Claude decides "not for me".** While something plays, the request Claude gets says so ("A film or music is playing in this room: Firefox."). Claude has a tool, `not_for_me`, to use only then, and only when what was heard is plainly speech from it rather than a request to Nova; when in doubt, it answers. Nova then says nothing, plays nothing, doesn't listen for a follow-up, and keeps the turn out of the conversation's thread. Logged at INFO ("Taken as speech from what's playing, so ignored"), with the words only if transcripts are kept. Called while nothing plays, the tool is ignored and Nova says "Sorry, I didn't catch that."
5. **Local commands still work.** Stop, pause, volume, timers and everything else answered without Claude, and Home Assistant's Assist, are untouched. Only what goes to Claude can be ignored. Follow-ups get the same check, as they're heard without the wake word. While something plays, if Claude can't be reached, nothing it would have been asked is acted on - Nova just says it can't reach Claude.

**What changes where.** Brain: a small media watcher beside the battery and device ones, the state message, the page's two settings, Claude's situation line and tool, and the session's silent end. Satellite: reads `wake_confidence` from the state message (checked: a number from 0.05 to 0.99, or ignored). The brain can go first: an older satellite ignores the new field, so Claude's check works before the Pi is updated.

**New devices and unknowns.** Any `media_player` HA puts in the room counts, with no list kept anywhere. One with no room never counts: Jellyfin's per-session players can't usefully be given one, which is why HASS.Agent's media player (on the PC's device, in the Bedroom) is the way in.

**Not now.** The voice check (planned 21 Oct) is separate and comes next.

**Hardware.** None of this is shaped by hardware.
